# 演示素材 / Demo assets

官网演示用的照片来自 Unsplash，遵循 [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)（可免费商用，不要求署名，这里仍然注明作者）。`lake.mp4` 是用 `lake.jpg` 在本地生成的缓慢推镜头片段。`video/zizai-zh.mp4`、`video/zizai-en.mp4` 是用上面的照片在本地逐帧渲染的介绍短片，背景音乐由程序现场合成，没有使用外部音频素材。

The demo photos on the website come from Unsplash under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). `lake.mp4` is a slow push-in generated locally from `lake.jpg`. `video/zizai-zh.mp4` and `video/zizai-en.mp4` are short intro films rendered locally, frame by frame, from the photos above; the music is synthesised in code, with no outside audio.
